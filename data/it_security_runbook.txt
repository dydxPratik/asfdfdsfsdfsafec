NEXAVAULT ROBOTICS INC. - INFORMATION SECURITY & INCIDENT RESPONSE RUNBOOK
Document Code: SEC-RUN-880 | Classification: Internal Mandatory | Revision: 2026.2

[SECTION 1: CREDENTIAL & HARDWARE STANDARDS]
1.1 All corporate laptops (macOS Sequoia or Ubuntu 24.04 LTS managed images) must enforce full-disk encryption (FileVault 2 or LUKS2) and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
1.2 Hardware security keys (YubiKey 5C NFC) are mandatory for SSH access to production robotics telemetry clusters (`prod-helios-eu` and `prod-titan-us`). SMS-based two-factor authentication is strictly prohibited.
1.3 Production API keys and service tokens must rotate every 45 days via VaultKeeper KMS. Hardcoding credentials in Git repositories triggers an automatic P1 revocation within 90 seconds.

[SECTION 2: INCIDENT SEVERITY & SLA TIMELINES]
- SEV-1 (Critical Fleet or Data Breach): Active compromise of autonomous warehouse units, customer telemetry leak, or ransomware detection.
  * Initial Incident Commander page: within 10 minutes via PagerDuty channel `#sec-sev1-warroom`.
  * Executive & Legal notification (`ciso@nexavault.internal`, General Counsel): within 30 minutes.
  * External customer notification (if customer data impacted): within 24 hours.
- SEV-2 (High Infrastructure Risk): Stolen unreturned laptop, compromised vendor token, or anomalous VPN login from restricted jurisdiction.
  * Triage SLA: within 1 hour.
  * Device remote-wipe execution via Kandji/FleetDM: within 2 hours of report.

[SECTION 3: AI & EXTERNAL LLM USAGE POLICY - CODE AI-GOV-12]
Employees are prohibited from pasting proprietary actuator firmware source code (`repo: nv-kinematics-core`), customer warehouse floorplans, or personally identifiable employee records into unapproved public consumer chatbots.
Approved internal inference gateways:
  - NexaVault Local RAG Workbench (on-device embeddings + approved enterprise inference gateway)
  - Project Helios Sandbox (`helios-llm.nexavault.internal`)
Violations of AI-GOV-12 require mandatory retraining and security review with the CISO office.
