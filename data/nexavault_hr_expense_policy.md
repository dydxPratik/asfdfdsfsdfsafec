# NexaVault Robotics Inc. — Global HR & Expense Policy Manual
**Document ID:** POL-HR-2026-V4  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026  
**Policy Owner:** People Operations & Finance (`people-ops@nexavault.internal`)

## 1. Hybrid Work & Core Hours (Section POL-WRK-101)
NexaVault Robotics operates on a structured hybrid schedule for all full-time employees at Grade L3 through L7.
- Employees must work on-site at their assigned hub (Austin HQ, Zurich Lab, or Bengaluru R&D Center) on **Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays**.
- **Core collaboration hours** are **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local hub time**.
- Fully remote exceptions require written sign-off from both a **VP-level Engineering Lead** and the **Director of People Operations** via form `FORM-REM-09`.
- Every employee receives up to **20 "Work-From-Anywhere" (WFA) business days** per calendar year, limited to countries on the Approved Tax & Export Control List.

## 2. Travel & Expense Reimbursement (Section POL-EXP-402)
All business expenses must be submitted in the **VaultSpend** portal within **21 calendar days** of the transaction date. Receipts are mandatory for any single line item exceeding **$25.00 USD** (or local currency equivalent).

### 2.1 Daily Meal & Lodging Caps
- **Tier 1 Cities (San Francisco, New York, Zurich, London, Tokyo):**
  - Lodging cap: **$340 USD / night** (before taxes and mandatory hotel fees)
  - Daily meal allowance: **$85 USD / day** (alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable unless part of a pre-approved client dinner under code `CLI-ENT-77`)
- **Tier 2 Cities (Austin, Chicago, Toronto, Berlin, Bengaluru):**
  - Lodging cap: **$230 USD / night**
  - Daily meal allowance: **$60 USD / day**

### 2.2 Home Office & Ergonomics Stipend (Code STIP-ERG-500)
New hires receive a one-time **$750 USD** ergonomic home-office stipend upon completing their first **30 days** of employment. Eligible items include external monitors, mechanical keyboards, standing desk converters, and BIFMA-certified ergonomic chairs. Gaming consoles, decorative lighting, and smart home speakers are excluded.

## 3. Paid Time Off & Parental Leave (Section POL-PTO-305)
- **Annual Paid Time Off (PTO):** Full-time employees accrue **22 days** of PTO per year during years 1–3 of tenure, increasing to **27 days** starting on their 4th work anniversary. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum ceiling of **8 days** into Q1 of the following calendar year; carryover days expire on **March 31**.
- **Parental Leave:** Primary and secondary caregivers alike receive **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**, which may be taken continuously or in up to two blocks within **12 months** of birth or adoption placement.
- **Sabbatical Program (`SAB-7YR`):** Employees reaching **7 years** of continuous full-time service qualify for a **4-week paid sabbatical** in addition to standard annual PTO.
